MOSAIC

Welcome to Mosaic

Mosaic is an event conducted by ITSA that brings students together to participate, compete and explore their technical skills. This year, we look forward to welcoming more participants and making the event bigger and more engaging for everyone.

The event will include a variety of activities and challenges where students can test their technical knowledge, problem-solving abilities and creativity. Participants will get the opportunity to challenge themselves, compete with others, win exciting prizes and leave with memorable experiences.

Rules

  1. Registration is mandatory. All participants must register themselves before participating in the event. Participants who do not register will not be allowed to participate.
  2. All participants must provide the required details during registration.
  3. Please ensure that all the information provided is accurate and complete. Avoid providing inaccurate or misleading information.
  4. Participants are requested to follow the instructions given by the event organizers throughout the event.
  5. Please help us maintain a respectful and welcoming environment so that everyone can participate properly and enjoy the event.
  6. Participants are expected to maintain proper discipline and cooperate with the organizers.
