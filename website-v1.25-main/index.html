EVENTS

Farewell and Welcome

This is the event where we bid farewell to our previous members and welcome the new members of the ITSA family. It is a celebration of the memories, achievements and contributions of our outgoing members while giving the new members a warm welcome into the ITSA community.

Study Abroad Seminar

This is a seminar where students gain insights into how and what to do to proceed with their studies abroad. Students can clear their doubts and understand the different opportunities available to them.

We also have one-to-one sessions where industry experts guide students through the required documents, procedures and other important steps involved in studying abroad.

E-Waste Drive

The E-Waste Drive is an open event where students and staff from across the college can responsibly discard their unused electronic items. ITSA collaborates with NSS SFIT to provide a proper channel for collecting and managing this.

By educating students about responsible disposal and recycling, we encourage everyone to adopt more sustainable practices when dealing with electronic waste.

CyberStorm

CyberStorm is a cybersecurity seminar organized by ITSA in collaboration with the team GhostNett. The session introduces students to important cybersecurity concepts and the different threats that exist in the digital world.

The seminar also includes a problem-solving and hands-on session, giving students a practical understanding of how cybersecurity works in real-world situations.

Infernox Drift Grand Prix

A fun and competitive event that brings students together for an exciting experience filled with creativity, competition and teamwork.

CodeSphere

CodeSphere focuses on learning and understanding Data Structures and Algorithms through platforms such as LeetCode. Students explore the importance of DSA in while also understanding how these skills can be useful when participating in hackathons.

The event includes coding challenges that help students develop their problem-solving and logical-thinking abilities.

Tug of Words 4.0

Tug of Words 4.0 is an open debate competition where students put their speaking and communication skills to the test. Participants have to think quickly, present strong points and express their ideas clearly while dealing with the pressure of limited time.

Designverse

Designverse gives students a hands-on introduction to Figma and the fundamentals of visual and interface design.

Through the event, students learn how Figma can be used to design websites and interfaces without having to write code, while exploring different aspects of modern web design.

ITSA-Hack 2.0

A hackathon where students come together to develop creative solutions, work in teams and apply their technical knowledge to solve real-world problems.
